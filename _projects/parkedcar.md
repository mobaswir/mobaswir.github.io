### Motivation
Traffic Jam has been one of the major issues in Dhaka, Bangladesh. And one of the main reasons for this is inadequate parking systems. ParkedCar system can help to manage parking system in a better way by availing the drivers to park their cars in unused parking spots from owners. Both the owners and driver will be benefitted and have a positive impact on reducing traffic jam. 
### Used Architecture, Pattern and Stack
- MVC architecture in backend.
- PostgreSQL for database.
- ExpressJS for server.
- Servers were hosted on render.
- Used MVVM pattern for Android Applications.
- Integrated Google maps for android apps.

### Features
- An owner can create space and mark free slots.
- A driver can browse free spots nearby and book for parking.
